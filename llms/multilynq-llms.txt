## What MultiLynq does

- One normalized API across venues, replacing per-venue integrations.
- Venue aggregation: pricing and market data from established and emerging venues
  over a single normalized API, with quoting, routing and RFQ options.
- Pre- and post-trade coverage: price dissemination, market data aggregation,
  smart order routing, post-trade reporting and reconciliation.
- Trading protocols handled: RFQ negotiation, portfolio trading, auction
  mechanisms, and various quoting conventions.
- Fully hosted delivery: the client establishes a single physical connection to a
  MultiLynq data center; MultiLynq maintains all upstream venue connectivity.
- Asset classes: corporate credit, municipal bonds, loans.

## Venues MultiLynq connects to

Bloomberg, MarketAxess (including Live Markets), Tradeweb (including Dealerweb
Wholesale and Tradeweb Direct), Trumid, ICE Bonds, LTX by Broadridge, Liquidnet,
Octaura, TMC Bonds, Bonds.com, UBS credit electronic trading.
